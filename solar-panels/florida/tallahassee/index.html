Solar panels in Tallahassee, FL: payback and output

A kW of south-facing panels in Tallahassee produces about 1,406 kWh a year, 9% less than Orlando, the Florida reference city. Summer months produce 1.2× what winter does. To cover a typical 10,500 kWh/yr home on paper you'd need roughly 7.5 kW.

What stands out in Tallahassee

  • Tallahassee ranks 50th of 98 cities for solar output (1,406 kWh per kW a year).
  • It produces 10% less than its latitude (30.4°N) alone would suggest, a sign of cloud, haze or coastal weather rather than distance from the equator.
  • The closest other city in our data is Jacksonville, FL (156 miles away): it makes 1,455 kWh per kW, 3% more than Tallahassee.
  • A 7 kW system here makes about 991 kWh in May and 678 kWh in December; at 14.74¢/kWh that is roughly $146 vs. $100 of electricity in those months.
  • Of the 5 Florida cities we cover, Tallahassee ranks 5th for output (Tampa, Fort Myers, Miami, Jacksonville and Tallahassee, sunniest first).

Solar panels in Richmond, VA: payback and output

A kW of south-facing panels in Richmond produces about 1,344 kWh a year, 6% less than Virginia Beach, the Virginia reference city. Summer months produce 1.6× what winter does. To cover a typical 10,500 kWh/yr home on paper you'd need roughly 7.8 kW.

How much do solar panels cost in Richmond?

A 8.0 kW system — about what a Richmond home using 10,500 kWh a year needs — costs roughly $24,000–$41,600 before incentives, with a midpoint of $32,000. That uses the national range Berkeley Lab measured for home systems installed in 2024 ($3.00–$5.20 per watt; median $4.00). Cash purchases had a median of $3.50/W and loan-financed systems $4.70/W, mostly because of dealer fees.

Incentives: the 30% federal residential credit no longer applies to systems placed in service after December 31, 2025 (IRS). State, local and utility rebates differ — check DSIRE for programs that cover Richmond, then compare at least a few local quotes.

Source: Berkeley Lab, U.S. Distributed Solar and Storage: 2025 Data Update (2024$/W, host-owned systems). Local prices vary with installer, roof and equipment.

Solar in Richmond: common questions

Are solar panels worth it in Richmond, VA?

For an example 7 kW system costing $24,500 on a home using 10,500 kWh a year, payback in Richmond is 13.7 years with full net metering and 18.2 years if exports earn half the retail rate, at the Virginia average price of 17.64¢/kWh (EIA, August 2026).

How much electricity do solar panels produce in Richmond?

About 1,344 kWh per year for each kW of south-facing panels at a 20° pitch (NLR PVWatts, typical-year weather), from 78 kWh in Dec to 140 kWh in Jul.

What size solar system does a home in Richmond need?

About 7.8 kW to cover 10,500 kWh a year. Divide your own annual use by 1,344 kWh per kW for a first estimate; if exports earn less than retail, a smaller system often pays back faster.

How much do solar panels cost in Richmond?

Roughly $24,000–$41,600 before incentives for a 8.0 kW system, using the national range Berkeley Lab reports for home systems installed in 2024 ($3.00–$5.20 per watt). Get local quotes to see where Richmond prices fall.

Does roof direction matter in Richmond?

Yes. Per kW at a 20° pitch: south 1,344 kWh, southeast 1,291 kWh, southwest 1,285 kWh, east 1,154 kWh, west 1,145 kWh, east-west (split) 1,149 kWh a year. South-facing roofs produce the most; east or west roofs still work, with less output.

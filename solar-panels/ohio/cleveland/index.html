Solar panels in Cleveland, OH: payback and output

A kW of south-facing panels in Cleveland produces about 1,274 kWh a year, 3% less than Columbus, the Ohio reference city. Summer months produce 2.5× what winter does. To cover a typical 10,500 kWh/yr home on paper you'd need roughly 8.0 kW.

What stands out in Cleveland

  • Cleveland ranks 81st of 98 cities for solar output (1,274 kWh per kW a year).
  • It produces 7% less than its latitude (41.5°N) alone would suggest, a sign of cloud, haze or coastal weather rather than distance from the equator.
  • The closest other city in our data is Toledo, OH (96 miles away): it makes 1,304 kWh per kW, 2% more than Cleveland.
  • A 7 kW system here makes about 1,081 kWh in July and 321 kWh in December; at 19.38¢/kWh that is roughly $209 vs. $62 of electricity in those months.
  • Of the 3 Ohio cities we cover, Cleveland ranks 2nd for output (Toledo, Cleveland and Cincinnati, sunniest first).

Computed from NLR PVWatts output for every city we cover and the state's EIA price.

Solar production and payback by state

Sunshine, power prices and an example payback for every state, from public NLR and EIA data.

Output: 1 kW of south-facing panels at a 20° pitch in a reference city for each state (14% system losses, NLR PVWatts v8 (U.S. DOE National Laboratory of the Rockies, formerly NREL)). Price: August 2026 EIA residential average. Payback: example 7 kW system at $3,500/kW with full net metering, which new customers can no longer get in California and Hawaii and which Texas does not guarantee (each state page explains). Open a state for details and assumptions.

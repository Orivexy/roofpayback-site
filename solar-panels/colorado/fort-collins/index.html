Solar panels in Fort Collins, CO: payback and output

A kW of south-facing panels in Fort Collins produces about 1,468 kWh a year, 8% less than Denver, the Colorado reference city. Summer months produce 1.6× what winter does. To cover a typical 10,500 kWh/yr home on paper you'd need roughly 7.0 kW.

What stands out in Fort Collins

  • Fort Collins ranks 36th of 98 cities for solar output (1,468 kWh per kW a year).
  • It is 6% sunnier than its latitude (40.6°N) alone would suggest — clear skies, not just a southern location, drive the output here.
  • The closest other city in our data is Colorado Springs, CO (122 miles away): it makes 1,642 kWh per kW, 11% more than Fort Collins.
  • A 7 kW system here makes about 1,048 kWh in June and 598 kWh in December; at 17.17¢/kWh that is roughly $180 vs. $103 of electricity in those months.
  • Of the 2 Colorado cities we cover, Fort Collins ranks 2nd for output (Colorado Springs and Fort Collins, sunniest first).

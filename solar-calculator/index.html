How to read the result

  • First-year savings: what you stop paying for power you use directly, plus bill credits for power you export.
  • Payback: years until cumulative savings (minus maintenance) equal the installed cost minus incentives.
  • kWh that earn no credit: exports beyond a year of grid purchases. Most utilities zero out or pay very little for excess credits at the annual true-up, so oversizing rarely pays.

The model, data sources and limits are explained in the methodology. Find your state's numbers on the state pages.

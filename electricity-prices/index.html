Residential electricity prices by state — September 2026

U.S. homes paid an average of 18.17¢/kWh in September 2026, up 0.7% from a year earlier. The most expensive: Hawaii (48.31¢), Maine (31.29¢) and Massachusetts (30.97¢). The cheapest: Nevada (13.25¢), Tennessee (13.29¢) and Louisiana (13.33¢). The biggest year-over-year increase was in Hawaii (22.2%).

Counting how much power homes actually use, the average residential bill was $160 a month from October 2025 to September 2026, ranging from $102 in New Mexico to $232 in Hawaii (EIA-861M). Each state page charts its bill month by month and its price since 2016.

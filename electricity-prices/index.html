Residential electricity prices by state — August 2026

U.S. homes paid an average of 18.16¢/kWh in August 2026, up 3.4% from a year earlier. The most expensive: Hawaii (47.18¢), Maine (32.11¢) and Massachusetts (30.48¢). The cheapest: Nevada (12.72¢), Louisiana (12.87¢) and Utah (13.31¢). The biggest year-over-year increase was in Hawaii (22.5%).

Counting how much power homes actually use, the average residential bill was $158 a month from September 2025 to August 2026, ranging from $100 in New Mexico to $228 in Hawaii (EIA-861M). Each state page charts its bill month by month and its price since 2016.

Residential electricity prices by state — August 2026

U.S. homes paid an average of 18.16¢/kWh in August 2026, up 3.4% from a year earlier. The most expensive: Hawaii (47.18¢), Maine (32.11¢), Massachusetts (30.48¢). The cheapest: Nevada (12.72¢), Louisiana (12.87¢), Utah (13.31¢). The biggest year-over-year increase was in Hawaii (22.5%).

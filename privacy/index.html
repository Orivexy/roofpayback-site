Privacy policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who we are

RoofPayback is operated by Eric Muñiz Genebriera, established in Spain (EU). Contact: ericmunizgeneb [at] gmail.com. Because the operator is in the EU, we follow the GDPR for all visitors, and we also describe the choices U.S. state privacy laws give you.

What we collect

  • Calculator inputs (location, usage, prices) are processed only in your browser and never sent to us. If you use "Copy link", those values are in the URL you share.
  • Email you send us: your address and message, used only to reply; kept up to 12 months.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics 4): pages viewed, device, approximate location and interactions, to learn which pages are useful. On the solar calculator we also record when it is used, shared, downloaded or printed, with the state chosen; the numbers you enter (bill, usage, costs) are not sent. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first.
  • Advertising (Google AdSense): third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ad Settings, and opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalized ads are shown. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Newsletter (if you subscribe): your email address, to send the newsletter. It is stored by our email provider on our behalf and you can unsubscribe from any email; we do not sell it.
  • Affiliate and partner links: if you click a link to Amazon or a quotes partner, that site may use cookies to record that you came from us. We do not receive your name or what you buy. See how we make money.
  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and prevent abuse; Cloudflare provides the domain's DNS. We do not see or keep these server logs.

Your choices

  • Change analytics/ads consent at any time: . Full list in the cookie policy. You can also block cookies in your browser or use Google's Analytics opt-out.
  • We do not sell personal information for money. Personalized advertising by Google may count as "sharing" under some U.S. state laws; you can opt out through the privacy choices link above or with a Global Privacy Control signal, and through Google Ad Settings.
  • You can request access, correction or deletion of data we hold about you, or object to processing, by emailing us. EU/UK residents can also complain to their data protection authority.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

See also the terms of use. This policy reflects how the site actually works but is not legal advice.

Planning a system

Sizing, roof direction and pitch, seasons and panel aging — what to know before a quote.

Before asking for quotes it helps to know how big a system your home needs, how much a roof facing east or west gives up, what winter does to output and how panels age. These guides use PVWatts output for every state so you can check an installer's production estimate against an independent one.

Solar payback

Whether rooftop solar pays off: credits, net metering, costs and savings, with real numbers.

Payback is the number of years before a system's savings cover what it cost. Two things decide most of it: the price you pay for grid power and how your utility credits the power you export. These guides run the same example home through every state so the results can be compared, and show what changes when the credit, the price or the quote changes.

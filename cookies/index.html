Cookie policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This page lists the cookies and similar storage used on RoofPayback. Change your choice at any time: .

Strictly necessary

  • consent-v1 (local storage, RoofPayback): remembers whether you accepted or declined analytics. Kept until you clear it.

Analytics

  • _ga, _ga_* (Google Analytics 4): count visits and pages viewed with an anonymous ID. Up to 2 years. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland they are only set if you accept; elsewhere they are on by default and you can turn them off with the button above.

Advertising

  • Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies (for example __gads, __gpi, IDE) to show and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, ad cookies are only set after you agree in Google's consent message, which appears before any personalized ad; our own banner covers analytics. See how Google uses cookies.

Affiliate links

  • If you click a link to Amazon or a quotes partner, that site may set its own cookies to record the referral. See how we make money.

How to control cookies

Use the button above, block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out. More detail in the privacy policy.
